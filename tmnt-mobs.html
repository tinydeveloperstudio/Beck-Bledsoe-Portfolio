Description

The TMNT Mobs mod is a project I worked on in my first year at Milwaukee Area Technical College. It is focuses on gameplay design, technical writing, pixel art, and mod development & testing. I used Blockbench to create custom mob textures based on my mod design inspired by Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. I developed the mod using CurseForge and Optifine, and tested the mod in CurseForge.
