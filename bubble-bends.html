Description

Bubble Bends is a project I worked on in my final year while at Milwaukee Area Technical College for the 2025 Global Game Jame. This project focuses on gameplay design & development and highlights my development skills within a 48 hour period. Using Unity and C#, I developed the main functionality of the game. I implemented art created by my sister to have various sprite animations and levels. I created some sound effects myself and implemented other free ones.
